Sections: each plate is first cut across its length into sections no longer than the limit (easier to carry and handle), and every piece stays inside one section. Section cuts show as black dotted lines.

"Straight through-cuts" guarantees every cut runs edge to edge across the board, which is what a table saw or panel saw needs. It can use slightly more material.
